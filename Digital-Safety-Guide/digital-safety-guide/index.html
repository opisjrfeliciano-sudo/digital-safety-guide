Use AI as a helper,
not the final answer.

Small, thoughtful choices help you use AI tools with care. Choose a topic to explore.

Showing all tips.

01◈

Privacy

Keep private details private.

Don’t enter passwords, private conversations, personal records, or information that could identify you or someone else.

02✓

Check accuracy

Check important claims.

AI can make mistakes or sound certain when it’s wrong. Compare important answers with trustworthy sources.

03✎

Learning & integrity

Let AI support your learning.

Ask for explanations or ideas, then do your own thinking. Follow your teacher’s rules and acknowledge AI help when required.

04♡

Wellbeing

Use your own judgment.

AI can help you reflect, but it isn’t a person or a professional. Talk to someone you trust about important personal concerns.

05◎

Privacy

Review what you share.

Before submitting a prompt, remove names, locations, contact details, and other information that isn’t needed.

06⌕

Check accuracy

Look for more than one source.

For schoolwork or decisions that matter, verify dates, facts, and references instead of relying on one AI response.

07↗

Learning & integrity

Make the work your own.

Use AI output as a starting point. Check it, revise it in your own words, and be honest about how you used it.

08◷

Wellbeing

Take a break when you need one.

AI is a tool, not a replacement for rest, real conversations, or support from people you trust.

03 · Put it to the test

Spot the scam.

Read each fictional message and look for clues. Remember: text alone can’t prove whether a real message is genuine.
